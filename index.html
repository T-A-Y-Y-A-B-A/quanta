FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Talk to a human. We usually reply in under 5 minutes.

How long does setup take?

Most teams connect their first sources and see live dashboards in under 15 minutes. Historical data backfills automatically in the background, usually within a few hours.

Do I need engineers or SQL to use Quanta?

No. Connectors are no-code and Copilot answers questions in plain English. Power users can still drop into SQL or our API whenever they like.

How does the AI Copilot use my data?

Your data is only used to answer your team’s questions. It is never used to train shared models, and admins can turn Copilot off per workspace at any time.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade or switch between monthly and yearly billing whenever you like. We prorate the difference automatically.

Is my data secure?

Quanta is SOC 2 Type II certified, encrypts data at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3), and supports SSO/SAML, role-based access and EU or US data residency.

What happens when my trial ends?

You pick a plan or your workspace simply pauses. No surprise charges. Your dashboards and data are kept for 30 days in case you come back.
